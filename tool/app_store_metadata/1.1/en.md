# App Store Metadata — EN

Locale: English (`en`)

## APP NAME

EAST.

## SUBTITLE

Where Silence Speaks

## PROMOTIONAL TEXT

One piece of wisdom. Then twenty-four hours of space—to keep, reflect, or simply let it pass.

## DESCRIPTION

One quiet moment. Every day.

Pause.
Feel.
Ask from your heart.

After a short moment, EAST. offers one piece of wisdom. Then it leaves twenty-four hours of space before another can appear. No feed. No streak. Nothing to scroll.

Keep a wisdom when it stays with you. Add a private Reflection, revisit what you have Kept, and gather what remains in your Journal.

EAST. is available in 15 languages.

The daily ritual is free. Keeper is an optional one-time purchase that removes limits on keeping and reflecting, enables Journal PDF export, and supports EAST. Keeper never unlocks another daily wisdom.
