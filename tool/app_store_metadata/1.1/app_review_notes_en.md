Version 1.1 review notes

No account or sign-in is required.

Daily access:
- EAST. presents exactly one wisdom per rolling 24-hour period. The lock applies to all users.
- Keeper does not unlock additional wisdoms or bypass the 24-hour interval.
- The Home Screen widget and “Begin EAST.” App Intent only open the app. They enter the same ritual and do not bypass the daily lock.

In-app purchase:
- Keeper is a one-time non-consumable purchase.
- It removes the free limits on keeping wisdoms and writing Reflections, enables Journal PDF export, and supports EAST.
- To restore: open EAST. > tap the menu icon in the upper-right > Settings > Restore Purchases.
- To view the purchase: use the same Settings screen > Keeper.

Language:
- To change language: open EAST. > tap the menu icon in the upper-right > Settings > Language.
- Version 1.1 supports exactly 15 product languages.

Data and iCloud:
- Kept wisdoms, Reflections, and Journal data work without an account.
- Private iCloud sync is optional. It can be enabled or removed from Settings > iCloud Sync / Remove from iCloud.
- The daily ritual timer remains on the device and is not synced through iCloud.

No review credentials are required.
